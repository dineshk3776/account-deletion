Delete Your Account – SipScout

If you would like to delete your account and associated data, follow the steps below:

Option 1: From the App

  1. Open the app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap "Delete Account"

Option 2: Request via Email

Send a request to: support@sipscout.com

We will process your request within 7 days.

Data Deleted

  • Name
  • Email address
  • User profile data

Data Retained

Some data may be retained for legal purposes for up to 30 days.

After deletion, your account cannot be recovered.
